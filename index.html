Camila Zita · Integral designer

Hi! I’m Cami, an integral designer from Argentina.

Throughout my studies, I worked on projects in industrial design, graphic, UX/UI, immersive, and audio design, understanding how these disciplines coexist within integral design.

01

Wedding Identity

I worked on the creation of the visual identity for a wedding, from the invitation and menus to the signage throughout the space. I really enjoyed the whole process, especially seeing the designs come to life and the final results brought into reality.

02

Sevensnack

SevenSnack is based on the design brief of developing a kitchen product that optimizes food preparation processes, with the aim of making home cooking healthier, more efficient, and more practical.

03

UNICO

ÚNICO is a group project developed with four classmates. It started from the brief of designing a real UX/UI application that addresses a specific problem. We focused on the challenges faced by students who live far from the university and the limited availability and discomfort of public transportation. The project proposes an app that centralizes all the necessary information to create “pools,” enabling groups of students to share a single car and facilitate their daily commutes to and from Universidad Torcuato Di Tella — designed and fully programmed in Figma.

04

Axia System

AXIA starts from the brief of selecting a Sustainable Development Goal and identifying a related problem. The project addresses freshwater waste in Mar del Plata through a system that recovers greywater from sustainable showers and reuses it for irrigating the plants of the beachfront facilities.

A short video that communicates the core concept of AXIA, combining a clear narrative with a conceptual and future-oriented approach.

05

Immersive and audio design

This project is an immersive experience for which I was responsible for designing the audio, lighting, and visual content projected across four walls. I also produced a manual-style PDF documenting the project’s concepts, ideas, processes, and implementation.

Esc Audio · Camila Zita

06

Mapping

This project is an audiovisual exploration created through projection mapping onto a model made of cubes and three walls. Warmth and coldness — represented by red and blue — emerge independently, occupy the space and leave luminous traces. As both forces interact, they merge into magenta, symbolizing union and transformation.

07

VJ / Live visuals

This project focused on learning VJ performance using Resolume Arena and Resolume Alley. I chose “Dinamarca” by Rusowsky and developed the concept of finding pause within speed. The visual language uses black-and-white and violet imagery, combining fast and slow movements.

Dinamarca — live VJ · Camila Zita

08

Pulso Podcast

PULSO is an intimate and reflective exploration of sports from a human and cultural perspective, using Rocky as a symbol of effort, resilience, and emotional endurance. Across four episodes, three female athletes share personal experiences centered on the body as a space of struggle, expression, and identity.

Pulso Audio · Camila Zita

09

Infographics

A series of infographics developed as part of an Information Visualization course. One explores the work and cinematic style of Quentin Tarantino, while the other focuses on the Cabildo in Buenos Aires. Both emphasize clear data organization, visual storytelling, and communication of complex information.
